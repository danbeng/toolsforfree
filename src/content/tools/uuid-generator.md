title: UUID Generator

  - Open the page. A UUID v4 is generated automatically.
  - Copy the UUID with the Copy button.
  - Click Generate for another UUID.
faq:
  - question: Which UUID version is this?
    answer: v4 only. Values come from crypto.randomUUID() in your browser.
  - question: Is my data uploaded?
    answer: No. Generation runs locally in your browser. Nothing is uploaded.
  - question: Can I generate many UUIDs at once?
    answer: No. This is not a bulk generator. Click Generate each time you need another UUID.
