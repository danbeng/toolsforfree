title: Base64 Encode / Decode
description: Encode or decode Base64 in your browser. Nothing is uploaded.
intro: Convert text to Base64 or Base64 back to text locally. Choose encode or decode explicitly; nothing leaves this page.
howTo:
  - Choose Encode or Decode.
  - Paste text or Base64 into the input.
  - Copy the result with the Copy button.
faq:
  - question: Is my data uploaded?
    answer: No. Encoding and decoding run in your browser. Nothing is uploaded.
  - question: What is the difference between encode and decode?
    answer: Encode turns UTF-8 text into Base64. Decode turns Base64 back into text. Use the mode toggle; the tool does not guess.
  - question: Why do I see Invalid Base64?
    answer: Decode only accepts standard Base64. Characters outside the alphabet, bad padding, or a truncated string produce Invalid Base64.
