faq:

    answer: No. Conversion runs in your browser. Nothing is uploaded.
  - question: Does the URL slug keep Chinese characters?
    answer: Yes. The slug keeps Han letters such as 你好世界. It does not transliterate to pinyin. Latin accents like café become cafe.

    answer: Yes. Each row has its own Copy button, and the main Copy button copies all nine rows.
