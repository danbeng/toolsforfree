title: Hex / RGB / HSL Converter
description: Convert colors between hex, RGB, and HSL in your browser. Nothing is uploaded.
intro: Convert a color among hex, RGB, and HSL. Conversion runs locally; nothing is uploaded.
howTo:
  - Enter a hex color, RGB channels, or HSL values.
  - Read hex, rgb(), and hsl() in the output.
  - Copy the converted values with the Copy button.
faq:
  - question: Is my data uploaded?
    answer: No. Conversion runs in your browser. Nothing is uploaded.
  - question: What hex format is used?
    answer: Output hex is always six digits with a leading hash, lowercase, for example #ff0000. A leading hash on input is optional.
  - question: Why do I see Invalid color?
    answer: Non-hex characters, RGB outside 0–255, or HSL outside hue 0–360 and saturation/lightness 0–100 produce Invalid color. Empty fields show no error.
